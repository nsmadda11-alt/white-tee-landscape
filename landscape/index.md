# Market map

Focus on brands where a T-shirt is the central product or promise. Mark broader labels and substitutes as adjacent when they compete for the same use or budget. Geography is open; record the market for each observation.

## Segments to scan

| Segment | What to look for |
| --- | --- |
| Tee specialists | One hero tee, signature fit, or white-tee promise |
| T-shirt-led brands | Tees define the brand, even across colors or cuts |
| Premium basics | Fabric, construction, or longevity leads |
| Value / multipacks | Low unit price, bundles, or replenishment leads |
| Material / purpose-led | Sourcing or impact claims lead |
| Fit-led | Sizing system or fit solution leads |
| Adjacent alternatives | Undershirts, uniforms, retailers, marketplaces |

## Early comparison

The reviewed pages show three distinct selling frames. Fresh Clean Threads pairs fit options and multipack savings with a cotton-poly blend. ASKET emphasizes organic cotton, fabric weight, production origin, and three shirt lengths. perfectwhitetee presents a fitted tee with a recycled-cotton story. These are brand claims and page observations, not independent proof of performance. [Sources S-001–S-003](/research/)

The listed single-tee prices span $23 to $78 USD in the US on 2026-11-09; ASKET was $65. Fresh Clean Threads also showed a discounted all-white six-pack, while perfectwhitetee's white variant price was not separately confirmed. Shipping thresholds differ too. Treat this as an initial price range, not a like-for-like value ranking.

## What we’re learning

- **Customer needs:** Too early to generalize. Fit and comfort feature in all three brands' own product stories.
- **Offer choices:** Multipack savings, specified material and construction, and named fabric/fit identity are three different ways the brands explain value.
- **Evidence gaps:** Independent fit and wear feedback; opacity; shrinkage and pilling; return experience; full delivered cost; and exact perfectwhitetee material details.

Next: collect independent customer evidence and validate these claims across product pages, policies, and customer sources before drafting a brand brief.
