# White Tee Landscape

A concise research hub for brands built around a plain white T-shirt. First map the market and document competitors; defer our customer, brand, and site decisions until the evidence is in.

**Live research hub:** https://nsmadda11-alt.github.io/white-tee-landscape/

## Research

- [Market map](./landscape/) — define the competitive set and its segments.
- [Competitor profiles](./competitors/) — use the [profile template](./competitors/template.md) for each brand.
- [Source log](./research/) — keep evidence, dates, and caveats together.
- [Future work](./later/) — persona, brand direction, and site plan, intentionally deferred.

## Working principles

1. Record sources and checked dates.
2. Separate brand claims from verified facts and our interpretations.
3. Compare price, product, and policies on the same market and date.

## Current scope

**Product anchor:** plain white T-shirt, including brands whose core proposition or business is centered on one T-shirt or a narrow T-shirt range.

**Geography:** open. Record the market with every observation.

**Stage:** information collection. No recommendation or brand brief has been made.
