# White Tee Landscape

Research hub for brands built around a plain white T-shirt. The current job is to map the competitive landscape and collect evidence. Decisions about our customer, brand, and site come later.

## Start here

- [Market map](/landscape/) — define the competitive set and track patterns.
- [Competitor profiles](/competitors/) — use the [profile template](/competitors/template) for each brand.
- [Source log](/research/) — preserve evidence, dates, and caveats.
- [Future work](/later/) — capture later-stage documents without deciding their content now.

## Working principles

1. Cite the source and date for each important observation.
2. Mark whether a point is observed, claimed by the brand, or inferred.
3. Compare prices within the same market, noting currency, shipping, tax, and bundles.

## Current scope

**Product anchor:** plain white T-shirt, including brands whose core proposition or business is centered on one T-shirt or a narrow T-shirt range.

**Geography:** open. Record the market with every price and availability observation.

**Stage:** information collection. No recommendation or brand brief has been made.
